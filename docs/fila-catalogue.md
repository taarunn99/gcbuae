## 4. Flags, contradictions and typos (kept verbatim in source, do not propagate to site copy)

1. INDEX VS BODY, FINISHING PAGES: the guide index (page 4) lists MATT at page 30 and SATIN at page 31 under FINISHING. The body actually prints CLASSIC on page 30 and LONGLIFE on page 31. MATT and SATIN do not appear anywhere in the body. CLASSIC and LONGLIFE do not appear in the index. Treat the body as authoritative; note that MATT and SATIN exist in the wider FILA range (MATT is cited on the Apple Store project reference) and may be quoted by FILA reps.
2. INDEX VS BODY, CONCRETE PAGES: index lists CONCRETE HYDRO 16 and CONCRETE SHIELD 17; body prints CONCRETE SHIELD on 16 and CONCRETE HYDRO on 17. Swapped.
3. Apple Store Piazza Liberty product list prints "LEANER PRO" (Profile 21). Almost certainly CLEANER PRO with a dropped C. Do not publish "LEANER PRO".
4. Company Profile page 9, 2021 timeline entry ends with a stray "Lorem ipsum" placeholder after "phosphate-free formula". Publishing defect in the source; exclude.
5. Values page prints "HUMILTY" twice (Profile 12-13); correct spelling is HUMILITY. "WHOLISTIC" (Profile 14) is a nonstandard spelling of HOLISTIC. Keep out of site copy.
6. Corporate Social Responsibility: "Care for People" and "Care for Territory" carry identical body text ("We believe it is our responsibility to support the areas where our business has its roots"), a copy duplication defect (Profile 38).
7. Text layer artifact: "agglomerate/agglomerates" extracts as "alomerate/alomerates" in two places in the profile text layer (Profile 17, 25); visually the print is fine. Extraction artifact only.
8. Several before/after photos retain Italian labels PRIMA DOPO in the artwork (NOSPOT, STONEPLUS, STOP DIRT, WET ECO pages). If reused on site, overlay English BEFORE and AFTER.
9. NORUST surfaces line ends with a trailing comma in print ("terracotta, concrete,").
10. CONTRACTOR'S CHOICE surfaces subtitle repeats the CONCRETE SHIELD surfaces list while its own label art says STONE & GROUT SEALER for natural stone, cement grout, concrete, terracotta. Likely a copy paste from page 16. Use the What it's for bullets as the authoritative use case (stone and grout).
11. Packaging decimal style is European in one place: "3,78 litres" (Contractor's Choice, a US gallon). Convert display to "3.78 L (1 US gal)" on site.
12. Water based share: profile body says "more than 83% of our products" while the stat block prints "+80% Water-based products" (Profile 33). Use "more than 83%" with +80% as the rounded badge.
13. Team photos on Profile 39 are captioned "FILA training in the 1980s" but show modern scenes with current branding; caption reuse defect. Do not reuse captions.
14. MP90 ECO XTREME application conditions print "temperature = 20 C" where the intent is approximately 20 C (the equals sign is a typographic simplification). Keep the printed form in spec tables, clarify in prose.
15. No prices, no TDS values (pH, density, VOC g/L), no SDS data are printed.
